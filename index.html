ABOUT THE PROJECT

Digital law should be
part of product thinking.

DevLex is a legal-tech concept designed to connect digital legislation with the day-to-day decisions of developers, founders, and product teams. It aims to make legal sources easier to discover and their technical implications easier to discuss.

✳

Our promise: clearer explanations, traceable sources, and practical questions — never a promise of automatic legal compliance.
